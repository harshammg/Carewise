FAQ

Common questions about the centre

Is a doctor available at night?

Yes. Nursing staff are on duty 24/7 and a doctor is on call at all times, with scheduled rounds every day.

Can a family member stay overnight?

Private rooms include a dedicated attendant bed so one family member can stay with the patient.

Do you accept ventilator-dependent patients?

Yes. We provide ventilator, BiPAP and CPAP support with trained respiratory care staff.

How are meals handled?

Meals are prepared in-house to the diet plan set by our nutritionist and the treating doctor.

Would you like to visit the centre?

Book a walk-through with our care coordinator at a time that suits you.
