FAQ

Questions about care at home

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Mysuru city and nearby areas. Contact us to confirm availability for your location.

Can I book a single nurse visit?

Yes. Single visits for injections, dressing or sample collection are available, as well as ongoing care.

How quickly can care start?

In most cases within 24 to 48 hours of the home assessment.

What if the condition changes?

Our doctor reviews the plan and, if needed, we arrange a smooth transfer to the Carewise Centre.

Need care at home this week?

Call us and a care coordinator will arrange an assessment.
