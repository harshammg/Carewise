Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

Carewise Healthcare ("Carewise", "we", "us") operates the Carewise Centre in Mysuru, Karnataka and a home healthcare service. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website and our services.

Information we collect

Contact details you share through our forms, phone or WhatsApp, such as name, phone number and email.

Health information about a patient that you choose to share so we can recommend and provide care. We treat this as sensitive personal data.

Basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited, used only to keep the website working well.

How we use it

To respond to enquiries, assess care needs, provide and coordinate care, send updates to the family, and meet legal and medical record-keeping obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

Information is shared only with the Carewise clinical team involved in care, with service providers who help us run the website or communications under confidentiality terms, or where the law requires.

Retention and security

Medical records are kept for the period required by Indian law. Other information is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent, by emailing support@carewise.in. We will respond within a reasonable time, subject to legal record-keeping duties.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@carewise.in or +91 76194 57863.
